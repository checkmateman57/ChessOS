# ChessOS

After looking in awe of people who created a WebOS, I am proud to say that I completed my own. <br>
Since I love chess so much, it is only natural that this WebOS be modelled around the game.

<h1> Apps on the OS </h1>
<b>A Welcome Screen</b>- I had made it while learning HTML and decided to keep it because why not. It's very basic though <br>
<b>Play a bot</b> - What kind of ChessOS would this be if you can't play a game of chess? You can choose different levels, get hints, take back moves, the whole deal. <br>
<b>Algebraic Notation (Notes App)</b> - A very simple app to take some notes that I created while learning HTML. <br>
<b>The Engine (Terminal)</b> - A chess themed terminal with a few commands. Don't know what they are? Then you are in <b><u>Stalemate</u></b> as what we like to say in chess.<br>
<b>Hall of Fame (Photo Gallery)</b> - I have 30 chess trophies. While I could do the ultimate flex and show them all off, that's too many pictures to take. Therefore, I took pictures of the most important trophies to me and put them on the photo gallery. <br>

<h1> Features: </h1>

Left &#9823; - Opens up the welcome screen to the OS <br>
Right &#9823; - Opens up a screen to play a chess game against a bo <br>
&#9820; - Closes the window <br>
&#9819; - Minimizes the window <br>
&#9818; - Maximizes the window <br>
As with anything with tabs/windows open, you can drag them along the top bar wherever you want.
